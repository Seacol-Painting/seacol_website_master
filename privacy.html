Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SEACOL PAINTING LLC uses this website to provide information about painting services and make it easier for visitors to request an estimate.

Estimate requests

When you submit the estimate form, we receive your name, email address, optional phone number, city, project type, property type and project description. We use these details to respond to your request, discuss your project and keep a record of our communication. Please do not include payment information, identification documents or other sensitive information in the form.

Requests are transmitted over HTTPS and stored in private storage provided by Vercel. These records are accessible to authorized SEACOL administrators. We do not publish your request or sell your contact information. We keep project inquiries for as long as needed to respond and manage the related work, and you can contact us to request access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Calls, emails and text messages

When you contact SEACOL by phone, email or text, your provider may also process your contact details and message under its own policies. The website includes email and text links as an alternative way to contact us.

Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand which pages, galleries and sections visitors view, which contact links they use, and how often estimate requests are started and successfully received. We do not send the contents of your form, contact details or request reference to analytics. Our tracking removes URL query strings and does not use advertising cookies. It respects browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.

Analytics and hosting may process technical information such as browser type, device category, approximate region and page activity. This website does not use advertising trackers or sell analytics data. A click on a phone or email link indicates interest; it does not confirm that a call, email or sale took place.

Security and reliability

Vercel processes hosting logs to operate and protect the website. The estimate endpoint uses validation, a spam trap and rate limiting. Rate limiting uses a protected hash derived from the network address; the raw address is not saved in estimate records.
