More about SEACOL

SEACOL PAINTING LLC serves homeowners, property managers and businesses throughout Seattle and King County. We bring the same attention to a single cabinet, a full home repaint and a commercial space: understand the project, prepare the surfaces carefully and give every detail the attention it deserves.

Your experience matters as much as the finished surface. We discuss the scope and finish expectations with you, protect the surrounding space and keep you informed as the work progresses. Respect for your property and clear communication guide the way we work.

From walls and trim to cabinetry and architectural woodwork, we select brush, roller or spray application to suit the surface. Repairs, sanding, caulking and priming form the foundation for the finish. We close with a detailed inspection, touch-ups and an organized cleanup.

Our standard is simple: careful craftsmanship, honest communication and pride in the work we leave behind.

Our process

Protection is not an extra. It is part of the finish.

  1. 01

    Consultation & Scope

    We review surfaces, access, repairs, product requirements and the desired finish.

  2. 02

    Protection & Preparation

    Floors, fixtures and adjacent areas are protected before repairs, sanding and priming.

  3. 03

    Professional Application

    Brush, roll or spray methods are selected according to the surface and finish standard.

  4. 04

    Inspection & Clean Closeout

    We inspect details, complete touch-ups, remove protection and leave the project organized.
